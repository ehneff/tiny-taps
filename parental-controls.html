Android — Screen Pinning

  1. Settings → Security & Privacy → More security settings → Screen pinning → turn on.
  2. Open Neffster, open Recent Apps, tap the app's icon at the top of its card, choose Pin.
  3. To unpin: hold Back + Recent Apps together (or enter your PIN if required).

iPhone — Guided Access

  1. Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn on, set a passcode.
  2. Open Neffster, triple-click the side/home button to start Guided Access.
  3. To exit: triple-click again and enter your passcode.

In-game pause

In Soft Bubbles, long-press the bottom-right corner of the screen for 3 seconds to pause or resume bubble spawning.
